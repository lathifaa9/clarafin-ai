# Clarafin demo dataset

The files in this folder form a single fictional SME financial-data set ending on 25 July 2026. Upload all current-period files together for the most complete source-aware analysis.

| File | Content | Reconciliation point |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `bank_statement.csv` | Cash transactions from May through 25 July 2026 | Transaction-level cash activity and anomaly examples |
| `open_invoices.csv` | Invoice register, including paid and unpaid invoices | The four unpaid invoices total $40,500 |
| `accounts_receivable_aging.csv` | Outstanding-invoice aging as of 25 July 2026 | Total outstanding balance is $40,500, matching July accounts receivable |
| `accounts_payable.csv` | Open supplier bills as of 25 July 2026 | Total outstanding balance is $8,200, matching July accounts payable |
| `profit_loss.xlsx` | Monthly profit and loss statement for May--July 2026 | Revenue, COGS, operating expenses, and margin trend |
| `balance_sheet.xlsx` | Monthly balance sheets through 24 July 2026 | July A/R is $40,500 and A/P is $8,200 |
| `cash_flow.xlsx` | Monthly cash-flow statement for May--July 2026 | Operating, investing, and financing cash movements |
| `Profit and loss.csv` | Original historical P&L supplied in `archive.zip` | Separate legacy data: 2019--2022, not part of the 2026 demo scenario |

All records are fictional and are intended for product demonstration and testing only.
